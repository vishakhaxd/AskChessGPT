Sample Question
Why is `Nf3` stronger than grabbing the pawn?
How AskChessGPT Responds

`Nf3` develops with tempo, supports central control, and keeps your king safe. The pawn grab wins material now but gives Black easy development and open lines against your king. In this structure, time matters more than the pawn.

  • Improves king safety
  • Supports `d4` and castling
  • Avoids falling behind in development
